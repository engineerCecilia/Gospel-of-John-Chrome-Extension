# Privacy Policy

This Chrome extension is designed to provide a simple, distraction-free reading experience for the Gospel of John. It is intended to operate with a narrow and clearly defined purpose and does not collect personal information from users.

The extension does not track browsing activity, record search history, or monitor user behavior outside of the extension itself. It does not store personal data, account information, or browsing history on any server. No login, registration, or external account connection is required to use the extension.

The extension may access local content needed to display scripture passages and navigation tools within the browser, but this access is limited to the extension's core functionality. It does not use advertising networks, analytics tools, or third-party data-sharing services.

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with any third parties. If the extension is updated in the future to include additional features, any new permissions will be limited to what is necessary for that feature and will be clearly communicated in the relevant update documentation.

By installing and using this extension, users agree that it is used solely for reading and accessing the Gospel of John in a local, privacy-respecting manner. The extension may be removed at any time by the user through the Chrome extensions menu.
